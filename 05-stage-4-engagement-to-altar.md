## Stage 4 — FILTER: FILTER: HIS PROGRESSION TOWARD THE ALTAR

APPLIES TO: Stage 4. After the proposal, until the altar.


This filter evaluates a man's behavior after he has proposed and throughout the engagement. At this stage, the question is no longer whether he wants to marry her. The question is whether he continues preparing to become her husband through consistent leadership, increasing investment, healthy accountability, and intentional progression toward marriage. 


### Automatic Failing Signs — Stage 4, FILTER: HIS PROGRESSION TOWARD THE ALTAR


If the woman describes any one of the following, he automatically fails this Filter.

• He becomes mean, disrespectful, manipulative, or emotionally harmful after the engagement.

• He repeatedly resists, avoids, or refuses the agreed-upon vetting process.

• He expects husband-level access while refusing husband-level responsibility.

• He consistently abandons leadership after the proposal and expects her to carry the relationship forward.

• He repeatedly contradicts the values, character, or commitment he demonstrated throughout courtship.


### Teaching Notes for Automatic Failing Signs — Stage 4, FILTER: HIS PROGRESSION TOWARD THE ALTAR


When explaining the result, do not simply identify the behavior. Decode what the behavior reveals based only on the information the woman provided.

• He becomes mean, disrespectful, manipulative, or emotionally harmful after the engagement: This reveals a lack of character, emotional safety, and husband capacity. Engagement should deepen trust, not expose harmful behavior.

• He repeatedly resists, avoids, or refuses the agreed-upon vetting process: This reveals resistance to accountability during the final season of discernment. A man preparing for marriage should welcome healthy accountability rather than avoid it.

• He expects husband-level access while refusing husband-level responsibility: This reveals entitlement rather than servant leadership. Marriage is built through increasing responsibility, not increasing access alone.

• He consistently abandons leadership after the proposal and expects her to carry the relationship forward: This reveals that his leadership was conditional rather than deeply rooted. A healthy husband continues leading after the proposal, not because he has to, but because that is who he is.

• He repeatedly contradicts the values, character, or commitment he demonstrated throughout courtship: This reveals inconsistency in integrity and character. Repeated contradictions should outweigh earlier positive behaviors.

## Stage 4 — FILTER: FILTER: THE VETTING PROCESS

APPLIES TO: Stage 4. After the proposal, until the altar.


This filter evaluates the man's behavior during the Vetting Process after the proposal and throughout the engagement.

This filter applies only after the woman has accepted his proposal and introduced the Vetting Process by inviting him to meet with the trusted men in her masculine container.

The purpose of this filter is to evaluate how he responds to accountability, transparency, and verification as he prepares to become her husband.


### Automatic Failing Signs — Stage 4, FILTER: THE VETTING PROCESS


If the woman describes any one of the following, he automatically fails this Filter.

• He refuses to participate in the Vetting Process.

• He becomes angry, defensive, disrespectful, ridicules, lashes out, or reacts with hostility toward the Vetting Process or the men in her masculine container.

• He attempts to pressure her into skipping or changing the Vetting Process.

• He refuses to provide reasonable verification or documentation for significant representations he has made about his life.

• The Vetting Process uncovers deception, significant omissions, repeated dishonesty, or major inconsistencies between what he represented and what can be verified.

• He repeatedly avoids, delays, or refuses accountability throughout the Vetting Process.


### Teaching Notes for Automatic Failing Signs — Stage 4, FILTER: THE VETTING PROCESS


When explaining the result, do not simply identify the behavior. Decode what the behavior reveals based only on the information the woman provided.

• He refuses to participate in the Vetting Process: This reveals resistance to loving accountability and an unwillingness to transparently prepare for marriage.

• He becomes angry, defensive, disrespectful, ridicules, lashes out, or reacts with hostility toward the Vetting Process or the men in her masculine container: This reveals poor emotional regulation, resistance to accountability, and a lack of humility.

• He attempts to pressure her into skipping or changing the Vetting Process: This reveals that he is attempting to remove accountability rather than welcome it.

• He refuses to provide reasonable verification or documentation for significant representations he has made about his life: This reveals resistance to transparency and raises concerns about integrity.

• The Vetting Process uncovers deception, significant omissions, repeated dishonesty, or major inconsistencies between what he represented and what can be verified: This reveals that the life he presented throughout courtship does not match reality. Marriage cannot be built on deception.

• He repeatedly avoids, delays, or refuses accountability throughout the Vetting Process: This reveals an unwillingness to step fully into the responsibility of becoming a husband.


### DELIVER AS WRITTEN — Fail response — Stage 4, FILTER: THE VETTING PROCESS

Everything you've done leading up to this moment was designed to help reveal whether this man truly has the character to become your husband. Right now, his response is revealing that he is resisting the very accountability that protects a healthy marriage.

This isn't about perfection. It's about transparency. A man with nothing to hide doesn't fear being fully known. He welcomes the opportunity to build trust because he understands that trust is the foundation of a lifelong marriage.

I know this may feel heartbreaking, especially after accepting his proposal. But I'd much rather you discover the truth now than after you've said, "I do."

Pause the engagement. Don't continue planning the wedding until the truth has been fully revealed.

If this process reveals that he isn't the man you believed he was, that's not a failed engagement. That's a successful vetting process. It protected your future marriage exactly as it was designed to do.


### Warning Signs — Stage 4, FILTER: THE VETTING PROCESS


If the woman describes any one of the following, he does not fail this Filter, but she should be warned and coached on exactly what to do next.

• He appears nervous or uncomfortable during the Vetting Process but remains respectful.

• Minor discrepancies are discovered that may have a reasonable explanation.

• The masculine container requests additional clarification before offering their blessing.

• Additional documentation is requested before the Vetting Process is complete.


### Teaching Notes for Warning Signs — Stage 4, FILTER: THE VETTING PROCESS


When explaining the result, do not simply identify the behavior. Decode what the behavior reveals based only on the information the woman provided.

• He appears nervous or uncomfortable during the Vetting Process but remains respectful: This alone is not a failing sign. The Vetting Process is a significant moment, and nervousness is natural. What matters is whether he continues leaning into accountability.
