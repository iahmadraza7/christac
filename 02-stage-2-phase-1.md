## Stage 2: First Date → The No-Girlfriend Standard Conversation (when he asks her for exclusivity) 


### Purpose — Stage 2 Phase 1

 Stage 2 begins after the first date and continues until a man asks for exclusivity.
 The purpose of this stage is to evaluate whether a man can consistently lead, progress, and deepen a connection through intentional courtship.

**This stage is split into two phases.**

In Phase 1 (the first few dates), a healthy man naturally begins asking questions about her long-term relationship goals. He is determining whether they are aligned while continuing to lead through consistent dates. At this stage, he is not asking for exclusivity. He is simply gathering information about whether she is someone he could continue building toward. If he reaches the end of the first few dates without showing curiosity about her long-term relationship goals, this becomes a concern that should be evaluated in the applicable Phase 2 Filters.

In Phase 2 (after the first few dates until he asks for exclusivity and she delivers the No-Girlfriend Standard), a healthy man continues progressing the connection through consistent action. His effort, consistency, and investment naturally increase. He continues planning dates, following through, becoming more intentional, and showing growing curiosity about her life, values, and long-term vision. He is no longer simply determining whether he enjoys spending time with her. He is determining whether she is the woman he wants to build a future with. As a result, his behavior naturally progresses toward asking for exclusivity. His actions should consistently move the relationship forward rather than simply maintaining access to her.

Your role during this stage is to evaluate whether his behavior is revealing:
 • Healthy courtship pacing
 • Consistent effort and follow-through
 • Leadership in progression
 • Emotional capacity
 • Alignment with long-term marriage potential
