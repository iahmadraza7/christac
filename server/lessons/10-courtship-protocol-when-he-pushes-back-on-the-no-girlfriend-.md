COURTSHIP PROTOCOL: WHEN HE PUSHES BACK ON THE NO GIRLFRIEND STANDARD… OR ANY STANDARD

SLIDE 1 — THIS IS WHERE IT GETS REAL

Okay, so we need to talk about the moment where holding your No Girlfriend Standard gets a little harder.

Because it's one thing to communicate the standard.

It's another thing when you really like him... he really likes you... things are going well...

And then he pushes on it.

And he can push on it in a lot of different ways.

He might say,

“Why can't we just be together?”

Or,

“I just want you to be mine.”

Or,

“I just need a relationship first and I promise it won't be long before I propose.”

Or he might come at it packaged as emotional intelligence and say,

“So you were girlfriends with men before. Why is this different now?”

“What happened that made you decide this?”

“Are you saying you would never be a girlfriend again?”

And here's what I want you to understand.

This is the exact moment where your old programming is going to want to take over.

You're going to want him to understand.

You're going to want to explain yourself.

You might start telling him about your past relationships.

You might start explaining why you created this standard.

You might start reassuring him that you're not difficult.

You might even start showing him what an amazing partner you would be.

And it feels logical.

It feels like you're being mature... even loving.

But what you're actually doing is allowing him to push your standard, your own desires, for his own comfort.

And when a man does this, even slightly, he loses respect for you.

And men do not marry women they do not respect.

So you don't bend the standard.

You don't explain the standard.

And you don't negotiate with the standard.

Because his discomfort does not make your standard less true.

His response is information.

Because the words matter.

SLIDE 3 — WHAT YOU ACTUALLY SAY

So let's make this really practical.

If he asks you,

“So you would never be my girlfriend?”

You can simply say, with a smile, of course,

“I'm not looking to be any man's girlfriend. I'm dating for marriage. I will be exclusive when the right man proposes.”

And then stop.

Not,

“I will be exclusive when you propose.”

When the right man proposes.

Massive difference.

You are not going to fill the silence.

You are not going to explain your childhood.

You are definitely not giving him the history of every relationship you've ever had.

You don't need to explain why your ex wasn't capable of giving you what you wanted...

or how he got comfortable...

or didn't rise...

or took you for granted.

He doesn't need your wounds in order to understand that you are not bending your standard.

He just needs to know you mean it.

And if he says,

“But I really want to be with you.”

Receive that.

Smile and say,

“I feel so flattered. It feels really good to hear that.”

That's it.

You don't need to keep repeating yourself.

You already said your standard again.

Now you're basically just being sweet because he's being sweet.

Remember, he's not saying,

“But I really want to marry you. Will you marry me?”

He's saying,

“But I really want to be with you.”

That's it.

And I want you to notice what you're doing in every response, no matter what.

You're never making this about him.

You're not saying,

“I can't wait for you to propose.”

You're not asking him to promise you anything.

You're not telling him what he needs to do.

You're talking about your life.

Your future.

Your desire to be a wife.

You're keeping your options open until the right man proposes.

That's what keeps you anchored.

Because you're not sitting there thinking,

“How do I get this man to become my husband?”

You're thinking,

“I'm already a wife. Becoming a wife is inevitable. I know what I want. Let's see what man gets the opportunity to be my husband.”

That is a completely different position.

Warm.

And then you stop.

And observe how he behaves after.

SLIDE 5 — DON'T TAKE OVER HIS PART

And this is the part I really want you to catch in yourself.

Because when a man pushes back on your standard, your instinct may be to make the conversation go well.

You want him to understand.

You want him to feel reassured.

You want him to feel good about you.

You want to make sure he knows you're still interested.

And so you start doing things that feel completely reasonable.

You answer every question.

You give more context.

You explain your thinking.

You reassure him.

You make the standard easier for him to swallow.

And you don't even realize you're doing it.

Because it feels like you're being emotionally mature.

It feels like you're communicating.

It feels like you're helping him understand.

But you're actually taking over his part.

His part is deciding what he wants.

His part is deciding whether he's willing to meet you there.

His part is deciding whether he's ready for the kind of courtship you're asking for.

That's not your part.

You already gave him the information.

You don't need to walk him all the way to the answer.

Let him think.

Let him feel.

Let him decide.

Because the second you start trying to manage his response, you stop observing it.

And that's the entire point of this standard.

You don't build the connection.

You watch if he does.

You don't create the momentum.

You watch if he creates it.

You don't convince him that you're worth pursuing.

You let him show you whether he is husband material

And this is where you become the match for what you say you want.

Not by trying harder.

By behaving like the woman who already knows what she requires.

You are not becoming her.

You're anchoring yourself into how she moves.

And that woman does not need to chase clarity.

She gives clarity...

and then she watches.

SLIDE 6 — BEFORE YOU GO ON THE DATE

And this is something I actually want you to come back to before you go on dates.

Because you don't know when a man is going to push on one of your standards.

So don't wait until you're sitting across from him to remember who you are.

Do this before the date.

Put your hand over your heart.

Close your eyes.

Take a breath.

And remind yourself:

“I deserve everything I've ever wanted in love.

I know what I want.

I do not have to abandon myself to be chosen.

I do not need to explain myself... or what I desire.

My standards are clarity.

The right man can meet me here.

I am not here to convince a man to choose me.

I am here to choose my husband.

The man who is right for me is already available to me.

I am already the woman who is pursued, adored, chosen, and married.

I am simply allowing the right man to reveal himself.

I don't build the connection.

I watch if he does.

I don't create the clarity.

I observe it.

I don't chase the outcome.

I allow the right man to show me who he is.”

And then you go on your date.

You don't go in thinking,

“How do I make this work?”

You go in thinking,

“Let's see who he is.”

That's the internal anchor.

Because the goal is not to hold a standard so tightly that you become rigid.

It's to hold it with so much certainty that you don't have to control what happens next.

You can be warm.

You can be open.

You can really like him.

And you can still let him reveal himself.

That's the woman who chooses.
