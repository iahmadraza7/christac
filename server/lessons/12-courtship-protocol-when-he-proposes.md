SLIDE 3 — RECEIVE THE PROPOSAL... THEN INTRODUCE THE FINAL VETTING PROCESS

By the time he proposes...

He has already demonstrated so much.

He's pursued you.

He's led.

He's been consistent.

He's chosen you over and over again.

And if you've followed this process...

This moment shouldn't feel rushed.

It should feel familiar.

Like everything he's consistently demonstrated has naturally led here.

So when he asks...

Receive him.

Receive the man who's chosen you.

Receive the moment.

Don't immediately jump into logistics.

Don't rush to planning the wedding.

Don't skip over the joy because you're already thinking about what's next.

Be present.

Receive his commitment with warmth.

Then...

After the excitement settles...

You introduce the final layer of your "yes."

Not because you're uncertain.

Because this is how you've chosen to build your marriage.

You simply say,

"This means so much to me. I feel really happy, and I want to marry you too. Before I can say yes fully, the next step is having the important men in my life get to know you. That's important to me moving forward”

Notice what you're not doing.

You're not asking permission.

You're not apologizing.

You're not hoping he's okay with it.

You're simply inviting him into the structure you've chosen for your future marriage.

And I want you to understand why this matters.

Not just for you.

For him.

Think about every stage you've already walked through together.

Every time there was another standard...

Another level of commitment...

Another opportunity for him to rise...

A healthy masculine man didn't pull away.

He leaned in.

Because men value what requires something of them.

Investment creates attachment.

Responsibility creates ownership.

Accountability deepens commitment.

This final season is no different.

Engagement shouldn't be the moment a man exhales and thinks,

"I've got her now."

It should deepen his desire to become the husband he's promised he's going to be.

This final layer of accountability does exactly that.

It reminds him that you're deeply loved.

Deeply protected.

That becoming your husband is still something he has the privilege of earning.

Not because he's trying to convince your family to like him.

Because he's stepping into your world with the same integrity he's shown throughout your courtship.

A man who's serious about spending his life with you isn't threatened by that.

He welcomes it.

Because he isn't trying to avoid being known.

He's preparing to become your husband.

If he resists it...

Gets defensive...

Or suddenly wants to avoid the people who know you best...

That's information.

Not because it means the engagement is over.

Because discernment never stops gathering truth.

You've spent this entire program allowing a man to reveal himself.

That doesn't stop because there's a ring on your finger.

It continues through one final season of discernment.
