### Automatic Failing Signs — Stage 3, FINAL FILTER: THE PROPOSAL


If the woman describes any one of the following, he automatically fails this Filter.

• He becomes angry, defensive, or disrespectful when she expresses that she needs a little more time before giving her full "yes."

• He ridicules, dismisses, or minimizes the importance of involving the important people in her life.

• He refuses the final vetting process or becomes threatened by loving accountability.

• He attempts to manipulate, guilt, pressure, or emotionally punish her for maintaining her standards.

• He ridicules or minimizes involving the important people in her life: This reveals resistance to transparency and accountability.

• He refuses the final vetting process: This reveals discomfort with being fully known before marriage.
